Hey everyone, I’ve recently just started practising Korean lash lifts/glueless lash lifts. After doing a masterclass on it I went and brought the Noemi lash lifting kit with Cysteamine. The recommended timings are 10-14 for thick full lashes. The 2 clients I’ve had in for lash lifts, I’ve not get the best curl and got a small lift. Step 1+2 were on for over 25 mins which seems unreasonably long. It’s left me feeling confused on what’s the right thing to do. I’ve followed the steps to a T (apart from timings) Please see below pictures, tips and advice would be very welcome! Also recommendations for new Cysteamine solutions please
